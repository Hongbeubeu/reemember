# Infinitives — To + V

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Rất cao — dùng sau nhiều động từ, tính từ, danh từ và để diễn đạt mục đích.

## Infinitive Là Gì?

Infinitive thường có dạng `to + base verb`.

| Vai trò | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------|
| Sau động từ | I decided **to leave**. |
| Sau tính từ | She is happy **to help**. |
| Chỉ mục đích | I called **to ask** a question. |
| Sau danh từ | It was a mistake **to wait**. |

---

## Verb + To + V

| Verb | Ví dụ |
|------|-------|
| want | I want **to learn** Spanish. |
| hope | We hope **to see** you soon. |
| decide | She decided **to apply**. |
| plan | They plan **to move** next year. |
| promise | He promised **to call**. |
| refuse | She refused **to answer**. |
| agree | They agreed **to help**. |
| manage | I managed **to finish** on time. |
| afford | We can't afford **to buy** a car. |
| seem | He seems **to know** the answer. |

---

## Verb + Object + To + V

| Verb | Ví dụ |
|------|-------|
| ask | She asked me **to wait**. |
| tell | He told us **to be quiet**. |
| want | I want you **to listen**. |
| expect | They expect him **to arrive** early. |
| invite | She invited me **to join**. |
| teach | My father taught me **to swim**. |
| encourage | The coach encouraged us **to try** again. |
| allow | They allowed us **to enter**. |

---

## Infinitive Chỉ Mục Đích

| Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|-------|-------|
| để làm gì | I went to the shop **to buy** milk. |
| nhằm đạt mục tiêu | She studied hard **to pass** the exam. |
| có thể thay bằng `in order to` | He left early **in order to avoid** traffic. |

> Không dùng `for + V`: ❌ I went there for buy milk. → ✅ I went there to buy milk.

---

## Sau Tính Từ

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| `be + adjective + to V` | I'm happy **to help**. |
| `too + adjective + to V` | The box is too heavy **to lift**. |
| `adjective + enough + to V` | She is old enough **to drive**. |

---

## Bare Infinitive

Một số động từ dùng `V` không có `to`.

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| modal + V | You must **go** now. |
| let + object + V | Let me **help**. |
| make + object + V | The film made me **cry**. |
| help + object + V/to V | She helped me **carry** the bags. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| I decided **leaving**. | I decided **to leave**. | `decide + to V`. |
| She asked me **wait**. | She asked me **to wait**. | `ask + object + to V`. |
| I came here **for study**. | I came here **to study**. | Mục đích dùng `to V`. |
| You must **to go**. | You must **go**. | Sau modal dùng bare infinitive. |
