# Adverbial Clauses — Mệnh Đề Trạng Ngữ

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Cao — dùng để nói thời gian, lý do, tương phản, mục đích, điều kiện và kết quả.

## Adverbial Clause Là Gì?

Adverbial clause là mệnh đề phụ bổ sung thông tin cho mệnh đề chính.

| Loại | Liên từ thường gặp | Ví dụ |
|------|--------------------|-------|
| Time | when, while, before, after, as soon as, until | Call me **when you arrive**. |
| Reason | because, since, as | We left early **because it was late**. |
| Contrast | although, though, even though, while, whereas | **Although it was cold**, we went out. |
| Purpose | so that, in order that | Speak clearly **so that everyone can hear**. |
| Result | so...that, such...that | It was **so loud that** I couldn't sleep. |

---

## Time Clauses

Với tương lai, mệnh đề thời gian thường dùng Present Simple, không dùng `will`.

| Sai | Đúng |
|-----|------|
| Call me when you **will arrive**. | Call me when you **arrive**. |
| I'll wait until she **will finish**. | I'll wait until she **finishes**. |

Ví dụ:

- As soon as I **get** home, I'll call you.
- Before you **leave**, check the windows.

---

## Reason Clauses

| Liên từ | Sắc thái | Ví dụ |
|---------|----------|-------|
| because | lý do trực tiếp | I stayed home because I was sick. |
| since | vì / bởi vì, khi lý do đã rõ | Since you're here, let's start. |
| as | vì, trang trọng hơn | As it was late, we took a taxi. |

---

## Contrast Clauses

| Liên từ | Ví dụ |
|---------|-------|
| although / though | Although he was tired, he kept working. |
| even though | Even though it was expensive, she bought it. |
| while / whereas | I like tea, whereas my brother prefers coffee. |

---

## Purpose Và Result

| Loại | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|------|----------|-------|
| Purpose | so that + S + can/could/will/would | I left early so that I could catch the train. |
| Result | so + adjective/adverb + that | The room was so hot that we opened a window. |
| Result | such + noun phrase + that | It was such a good film that I watched it twice. |

---

## Vị Trí Và Dấu Phẩy

| Vị trí | Dấu phẩy | Ví dụ |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Clause phụ đứng đầu | thường dùng | When I arrived, everyone was waiting. |
| Clause phụ đứng sau | thường không dùng | Everyone was waiting when I arrived. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| When I will arrive, I will call you. | When I arrive, I will call you. | Time clause tương lai dùng Present Simple. |
| Although it was late, but we stayed. | Although it was late, we stayed. | Không dùng `although` với `but`. |
| The movie was so interesting that I watched it twice. | The movie was so interesting that I watched it twice. | Đúng: `so + adjective + that`. |
| It was so a good film that I cried. | It was such a good film that I cried. | Dùng `such + noun phrase + that`. |
