# Conjunctions — And / But / So / Because / Although

> **Cấp độ:** A2 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Rất cao — dùng để nối ý, thêm thông tin, đối lập, giải thích lý do và kết quả.

## Conjunction Là Gì?

Conjunction nối từ, cụm từ hoặc mệnh đề.

| Conjunction | Ý nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|-------------|---------|-------|
| and | thêm thông tin | She is kind **and** helpful. |
| but | đối lập | I was tired, **but** I kept working. |
| or | lựa chọn | Tea **or** coffee? |
| so | kết quả | It was late, **so** we went home. |
| because | lý do | We stayed home **because** it rained. |
| although | nhượng bộ / đối lập bất ngờ | **Although** it rained, we went out. |

---

## And / But / Or

| Từ | Dùng để | Ví dụ |
|----|--------|-------|
| and | nối ý cùng hướng | I cooked dinner and washed the dishes. |
| but | nối ý trái hướng | The test was hard, but I passed. |
| or | lựa chọn / khả năng khác | Call me or send an email. |

---

## So / Because

| Từ | Trọng tâm | Ví dụ |
|----|----------|-------|
| because | lý do | I left early because I was sick. |
| so | kết quả | I was sick, so I left early. |

> Không dùng `because` và `so` cùng lúc cho cùng một quan hệ: ❌ Because I was sick, so I left early.

---

## Although / But

`although` tạo mệnh đề phụ; `but` nối hai mệnh đề chính.

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| Although + clause, main clause | Although it was cold, we went swimming. |
| Main clause, but main clause | It was cold, but we went swimming. |

> Không dùng `although` và `but` cùng lúc: ❌ Although it was cold, but we went swimming.

---

## Dấu Phẩy

| Trường hợp | Ví dụ |
|------------|-------|
| Mệnh đề phụ đứng đầu câu | Because it rained, we stayed home. |
| Although đứng đầu câu | Although he was tired, he continued. |
| So/but nối hai mệnh đề chính | It was late, so we left. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| Because I was tired, so I slept. | Because I was tired, I slept. / I was tired, so I slept. | Không dùng cả `because` và `so`. |
| Although it was raining, but we left. | Although it was raining, we left. | Không dùng cả `although` và `but`. |
| I like tea, and coffee. | I like tea and coffee. | Không cần dấu phẩy khi chỉ nối hai danh từ ngắn. |
| He studied hard because passed. | He studied hard, so he passed. | `because` cần mệnh đề lý do đầy đủ. |
