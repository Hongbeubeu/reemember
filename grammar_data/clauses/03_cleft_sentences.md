# Cleft Sentences — It Is... / What...

> **Cấp độ:** C1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Trung bình — dùng để nhấn mạnh một phần cụ thể của câu trong văn nói trang trọng, văn viết và thuyết trình.

## Cleft Sentence Là Gì?

Cleft sentence tách một câu thành hai phần để nhấn mạnh thông tin.

| Câu thường | Cleft sentence |
|------------|----------------|
| Tom broke the window. | **It was Tom who** broke the window. |
| I need more time. | **What I need is** more time. |

---

## It-cleft

Cấu trúc:

`It + be + focused information + who/that/which + clause`

| Nhấn mạnh | Ví dụ |
|-----------|-------|
| người | **It was Anna who** called me. |
| vật | **It was the blue bag that** she bought. |
| thời gian | **It was yesterday that** we met. |
| nơi chốn | **It was in Paris that** they got married. |
| lý do | **It was because of the delay that** we missed the train. |

---

## What-cleft

Cấu trúc:

`What + clause + be + focused information`

| Câu thường | What-cleft |
|------------|------------|
| I need a break. | **What I need is** a break. |
| She wants more support. | **What she wants is** more support. |
| He did something surprising. | **What he did was** surprising. |

---

## All-cleft / The Thing Is...

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| All + clause + be + focus | **All I want is** a chance. |
| The thing/problem/reason is + clause | **The problem is that** we don't have enough time. |

---

## Thì Và Agreement

| Focus | Verb `be` | Ví dụ |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| hiện tại | is/are | It is the details that matter. |
| quá khứ | was/were | It was Jack who sent the email. |
| danh từ số nhiều trong what-cleft | are | What we need are clear rules. |

Trong văn nói, `What we need is clear rules` cũng gặp, nhưng văn viết cẩn thận thường dùng `are` với focus số nhiều.

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| It was Tom he broke the window. | It was Tom who broke the window. | Cần `who/that` nối mệnh đề. |
| What I need are a rest. | What I need is a rest. | Focus số ít → `is`. |
| It is yesterday when we met. | It was yesterday that we met. | Với nhấn mạnh thời gian trong it-cleft thường dùng `that`; thì quá khứ dùng `was`. |
| What she did was she left early. | What she did was leave early. | Sau `What someone did was` thường dùng base verb. |
