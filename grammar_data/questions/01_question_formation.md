# Question Formation — Cách Đặt Câu Hỏi

> **Cấp độ:** A2 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Rất cao — nền tảng để hỏi thông tin, xác nhận, giao tiếp hàng ngày và phỏng vấn.

## Cấu Trúc Cơ Bản

| Loại câu hỏi | Công thức | Ví dụ |
|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Yes/No question | `Auxiliary + S + main verb?` | **Do** you **like** coffee? |
| Wh-question | `Wh-word + auxiliary + S + main verb?` | **Where do** you **live**? |
| Be question | `Be + S + complement?` | **Are** you ready? |
| Modal question | `Modal + S + V?` | **Can** you swim? |

---

## Khi Nào Dùng `do / does / did`?

Dùng `do / does / did` khi câu khẳng định **không có** `be`, modal, hoặc auxiliary khác.

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| You work here. | **Do** you work here? |
| She likes tea. | **Does** she like tea? |
| They arrived late. | **Did** they arrive late? |

> Sau `does/did`, động từ chính trở về dạng nguyên mẫu: Does she **like**...? Did they **arrive**...?

---

## Câu Hỏi Với `be`

Không dùng `do` với `be`; đảo `be` lên trước chủ ngữ.

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| He is tired. | **Is** he tired? |
| They were at home. | **Were** they at home? |
| I am late. | **Am** I late? |

---

## Câu Hỏi Với Auxiliary Và Modal

| Statement | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| She has finished. | **Has** she finished? |
| They are working. | **Are** they working? |
| You can drive. | **Can** you drive? |
| He will call. | **Will** he call? |
| We should leave. | **Should** we leave? |

---

## Wh-questions

| Wh-word | Hỏi về | Ví dụ |
|---------|--------|-------|
| who | người | Who did you meet? |
| what | vật / việc | What do you need? |
| where | nơi chốn | Where are they going? |
| when | thời gian | When did she arrive? |
| why | lý do | Why are you upset? |
| how | cách thức | How do you spell it? |
| how often | tần suất | How often do you exercise? |
| how much/many | số lượng | How many people came? |

---

## Subject Questions

Khi `who/what` là **chủ ngữ**, không dùng `do/does/did`.

| Object Question | Subject Question |
|-----------------|------------------|
| Who did you call? | Who called you? |
| What did she break? | What broke? |
| Which team did they beat? | Which team won? |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| Where you live? | Where do you live? | Present Simple cần `do/does`. |
| Does she likes coffee? | Does she like coffee? | Sau `does`, dùng verb nguyên mẫu. |
| Did he went home? | Did he go home? | Sau `did`, dùng verb nguyên mẫu. |
| Do you are ready? | Are you ready? | Với `be`, đảo `be`, không dùng `do`. |
| Who did call you? | Who called you? | `who` là chủ ngữ nên không dùng `did`. |
