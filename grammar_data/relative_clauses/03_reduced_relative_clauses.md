# Reduced Relative Clauses — Rút Gọn Mệnh Đề Quan Hệ

> **Cấp độ:** B2 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Trung bình — thường gặp trong văn viết, mô tả sản phẩm, học thuật, báo chí và thông báo.

## Reduced Relative Clause Là Gì?

Reduced relative clause là cách rút gọn mệnh đề quan hệ khi có thể bỏ relative pronoun và dùng `V-ing`, `V3/ed`, hoặc `to V`.

| Full relative clause | Reduced |
|----------------------|---------|
| The man **who is standing** over there is my teacher. | The man **standing** over there is my teacher. |
| The files **which were sent** yesterday are missing. | The files **sent** yesterday are missing. |
| The first person **who arrives** will get a prize. | The first person **to arrive** will get a prize. |

---

## Rút Gọn Chủ Động: V-ing

Dùng `V-ing` khi noun **tự làm hành động**.

| Full | Reduced |
|------|---------|
| People **who live** nearby can apply. | People **living** nearby can apply. |
| The woman **who is talking** to Tom is my aunt. | The woman **talking** to Tom is my aunt. |
| Students **who want** more practice should join the club. | Students **wanting** more practice should join the club. |

---

## Rút Gọn Bị Động: V3/ed

Dùng `V3/ed` khi noun **nhận hành động**.

| Full | Reduced |
|------|---------|
| The report **which was written** by Mai is excellent. | The report **written** by Mai is excellent. |
| Products **that are made** locally are popular. | Products **made** locally are popular. |
| The man **who was injured** in the accident recovered. | The man **injured** in the accident recovered. |

---

## Rút Gọn Với `to V`

Dùng `to V` sau các từ như `first`, `last`, `next`, `only`, superlatives hoặc mục đích/khả năng.

| Full | Reduced |
|------|---------|
| She was the first person **who arrived**. | She was the first person **to arrive**. |
| He is the only student **who can solve** it. | He is the only student **to solve** it. |
| I need a quiet place **where I can study**. | I need a quiet place **to study**. |

---

## Khi Không Nên Rút Gọn

| Không nên rút gọn khi | Ví dụ |
|-----------------------|-------|
| Relative pronoun là object và mệnh đề đã đủ ngắn | The book **I bought** is good. |
| Có modal/tense cần giữ rõ nghĩa | The people **who will attend** must register. |
| Rút gọn gây mơ hồ hoặc thiếu tự nhiên | The idea **that he suggested** was useful. |

---

## So Sánh Với Participle Clauses

Reduced relative clause bổ nghĩa cho **danh từ ngay trước nó**.

- The woman **standing by the door** is my boss.

Participle clause thường bổ nghĩa cho **cả mệnh đề chính** hoặc chủ ngữ của câu.

- **Standing by the door**, she looked nervous.

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| The man **who standing** there is my uncle. | The man **standing** there is my uncle. | Rút gọn thì bỏ `who is`. |
| The documents **sending** yesterday are confidential. | The documents **sent** yesterday are confidential. | Tài liệu được gửi → dùng V3. |
| People **lived** nearby can apply. | People **living** nearby can apply. | Người tự sống ở đó → dùng V-ing. |
| She was the first person **arriving**. | She was the first person **to arrive**. | Sau `the first person` thường dùng `to V`. |
