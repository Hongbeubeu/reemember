# Defining Relative Clauses — Mệnh Đề Quan Hệ Xác Định

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Rất cao — dùng để xác định chính xác người/vật đang nói đến.

## Defining Relative Clause Là Gì?

Mệnh đề quan hệ xác định cung cấp thông tin **cần thiết** để biết người/vật nào được nhắc tới.

| Câu đơn | Câu có relative clause |
|---------|-------------------------|
| I met a woman. She speaks five languages. | I met a woman **who speaks five languages**. |
| This is the book. I told you about it. | This is the book **which I told you about**. |

> Defining relative clauses **không dùng dấu phẩy**.

---

## Đại Từ Quan Hệ

| Relative pronoun | Dùng cho | Ví dụ |
|------------------|----------|-------|
| who | người | The man **who lives next door** is a doctor. |
| which | vật / động vật | The phone **which I bought** is expensive. |
| that | người hoặc vật | The film **that we watched** was great. |
| whose | sở hữu | The girl **whose bike was stolen** is my friend. |
| where | nơi chốn | This is the cafe **where we met**. |
| when | thời gian | I remember the day **when we moved here**. |
| why | lý do | Tell me the reason **why you left**. |

---

## Subject Và Object Relative Pronouns

| Vai trò | Có thể bỏ không? | Ví dụ |
|--------|-------------------|-------|
| Subject | Không | The woman **who called** you is my boss. |
| Object | Có thể bỏ | The woman **who/that I called** is my boss. |

Object relative pronoun có thể bỏ:

- The book **that I borrowed** was useful.
- The book **I borrowed** was useful.

Subject relative pronoun không thể bỏ:

- ✅ The man **who helped me** was kind.
- ❌ The man helped me was kind.

---

## `who`, `which`, `that`

| Cách dùng | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| `who` cho người | The teacher **who explains clearly** is popular. |
| `which` cho vật | The laptop **which broke yesterday** is mine. |
| `that` cho người/vật trong defining clauses | The teacher **that explains clearly** is popular. |

> Trong defining clauses, `that` rất thông dụng. Trong non-defining clauses, không dùng `that`.

---

## Giới Từ Trong Relative Clauses

| Tự nhiên / văn nói | Trang trọng |
|--------------------|------------|
| The person **I spoke to** was helpful. | The person **to whom I spoke** was helpful. |
| The topic **we talked about** was interesting. | The topic **about which we talked** was interesting. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| The man **he** lives next door is a doctor. | The man **who** lives next door is a doctor. | Không dùng thêm pronoun làm chủ ngữ. |
| The book **which I bought it** is useful. | The book **which I bought** is useful. | Không lặp lại object `it`. |
| The woman helped me was kind. | The woman **who helped me** was kind. | Subject relative pronoun không được bỏ. |
| This is the place **which** we met. | This is the place **where** we met. | Dùng `where` cho nơi chốn nếu không có giới từ. |
