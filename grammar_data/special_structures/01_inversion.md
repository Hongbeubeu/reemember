# Inversion — Đảo Ngữ

> **Cấp độ:** C1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Trung bình — thường gặp trong văn viết trang trọng, nhấn mạnh, tin tức và bài thi nâng cao.

## Inversion Là Gì?

Inversion đảo auxiliary/modal lên trước chủ ngữ sau một số cụm phủ định hoặc giới hạn.

| Bình thường | Đảo ngữ |
|-------------|---------|
| I have never seen such a view. | **Never have I seen** such a view. |
| She rarely makes mistakes. | **Rarely does she make** mistakes. |

---

## Negative / Limiting Adverbs

| Cụm mở đầu | Ví dụ |
|------------|-------|
| never | **Never have I heard** that story. |
| rarely / seldom | **Rarely does he arrive** late. |
| hardly / scarcely | **Hardly had we arrived** when it started raining. |
| no sooner | **No sooner had she left** than he called. |
| not only | **Not only did he apologise**, but he also paid for the damage. |
| under no circumstances | **Under no circumstances should you open** this door. |
| at no time | **At no time did they explain** the delay. |

---

## Only + Time / Condition

| Cụm | Ví dụ |
|-----|-------|
| only then | **Only then did I understand** the problem. |
| only later | **Only later did she realise** the truth. |
| only after + clause | **Only after I called him did he reply**. |
| only if + clause | **Only if you practise will you improve**. |

> Khi `only after/if/when` đứng đầu, đảo ngữ ở **mệnh đề chính**, không phải mệnh đề ngay sau `only`.

---

## So / Such

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| so + adjective + be + S | **So tired was he** that he fell asleep immediately. |
| such + be + noun phrase | **Such was the pressure** that many people quit. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| Never I have seen this. | Never have I seen this. | Cần đảo auxiliary trước subject. |
| Rarely he makes mistakes. | Rarely does he make mistakes. | Present Simple cần `does + V`. |
| Only after did I read the report I understood. | Only after I read the report did I understand. | Đảo ở mệnh đề chính. |
| No sooner she had left than he arrived. | No sooner had she left than he arrived. | `No sooner` cần đảo với Past Perfect. |
