# Dependent Prepositions — Giới Từ Đi Kèm Động Từ Và Tính Từ

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Cao — nhiều động từ, tính từ và danh từ cần một giới từ cố định đi kèm.

## Dependent Prepositions Là Gì?

Dependent prepositions là giới từ đi theo một từ nhất định. Chúng thường phải học theo cụm.

| Cụm | Ví dụ |
|-----|-------|
| depend on | It depends **on** the weather. |
| interested in | She is interested **in** art. |
| good at | He is good **at** maths. |
| reason for | What is the reason **for** the delay? |

---

## Verb + Preposition

| Verb pattern | Ví dụ |
|--------------|-------|
| depend on | Success depends on practice. |
| listen to | Please listen to me. |
| wait for | I'm waiting for the bus. |
| look at | Look at this photo. |
| apologise for | She apologised for being late. |
| believe in | Do you believe in luck? |
| belong to | This bag belongs to me. |
| deal with | We need to deal with this issue. |
| suffer from | He suffers from back pain. |
| apply for | She applied for a scholarship. |

---

## Adjective + Preposition

| Adjective pattern | Ví dụ |
|-------------------|-------|
| afraid of | I'm afraid of spiders. |
| interested in | They are interested in history. |
| good/bad at | She is good at tennis. |
| proud of | He is proud of his son. |
| famous for | The city is famous for its food. |
| responsible for | Who is responsible for this project? |
| similar to | This word is similar to that one. |
| different from/to | My plan is different from yours. |
| married to | She is married to a doctor. |
| angry with someone / about something | He was angry with me about the mistake. |

---

## Noun + Preposition

| Noun pattern | Ví dụ |
|--------------|-------|
| reason for | The reason for the delay is unclear. |
| solution to | We need a solution to the problem. |
| increase in | There was an increase in sales. |
| demand for | There is high demand for housing. |
| effect on | Stress has an effect on health. |
| advantage of | What are the advantages of working remotely? |

---

## Sau Preposition Dùng Gì?

Sau preposition dùng noun, pronoun hoặc `V-ing`.

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|----------|-------|
| preposition + noun | interested in music |
| preposition + pronoun | listen to them |
| preposition + V-ing | apologise for being late |

> Không dùng `to V` sau preposition: ❌ interested in to learn → ✅ interested in learning.

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| depend of | depend on | Cụm đúng là `depend on`. |
| listen music | listen to music | `listen` cần `to`. |
| good in English | good at English | Kỹ năng dùng `good at`. |
| married with | married to | Cụm đúng là `married to`. |
| interested in learn | interested in learning | Sau preposition dùng `V-ing`. |
