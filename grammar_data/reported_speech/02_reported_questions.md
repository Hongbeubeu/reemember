# Reported Questions — Câu Hỏi Tường Thuật

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Cao — dùng để kể lại câu hỏi, yêu cầu thông tin hoặc thắc mắc của người khác.

## Quy Tắc Quan Trọng Nhất

Trong reported questions, câu hỏi trở thành **mệnh đề trần thuật**, nên không đảo trợ động từ.

| Direct Question | Reported Question |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| "Where **do you live**?" | She asked where I **lived**. |
| "What **are you doing**?" | He asked what I **was doing**. |
| "Can you help me?" | She asked if I **could help** her. |

> Không dùng dấu hỏi nếu toàn câu là câu kể: She asked where I lived.

---

## Wh-questions

Dùng lại từ hỏi: `what`, `where`, `when`, `why`, `who`, `whose`, `which`, `how`.

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Where do you work?" | He asked where I worked. |
| "Why did she leave?" | They asked why she had left. |
| "How long have you waited?" | She asked how long I had waited. |

Công thức:

`ask / wonder / want to know + wh-word + S + V`

---

## Yes/No Questions

Dùng `if` hoặc `whether`.

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| "Do you like coffee?" | She asked if I liked coffee. |
| "Are they coming?" | He asked whether they were coming. |
| "Have you seen this film?" | They asked if I had seen that film. |

`whether` thường trang trọng hơn và dùng tốt khi có `or not`.

---

## Lùi Thì Trong Reported Questions

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| do/does + V | Past Simple |
| am/is/are + V-ing | was/were + V-ing |
| did + V | had + V3 |
| have/has + V3 | had + V3 |
| can | could |
| will | would |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| She asked where did I live. | She asked where I lived. | Không đảo trợ động từ trong câu hỏi tường thuật. |
| He asked do I like tea. | He asked if I liked tea. | Yes/No question cần `if/whether`. |
| They asked where I live? | They asked where I lived. | Toàn câu là câu kể, không cần dấu hỏi. |
| She asked me where was Tom. | She asked me where Tom was. | Dùng thứ tự `S + V`, không đảo. |
