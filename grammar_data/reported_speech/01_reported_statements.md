# Reported Statements — Câu Tường Thuật Dạng Kể

> **Cấp độ:** B1 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Rất cao — dùng để kể lại lời nói, tin tức, ý kiến hoặc thông tin người khác đã nói.

## Cấu Trúc Chung

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| `"I am tired," she said.` | She said **that she was tired**. |
| `"We have finished," they said.` | They said **that they had finished**. |

`that` thường có thể bỏ trong văn nói:

- She said **that** she was tired.
- She said she was tired.

---

## Động Từ Tường Thuật

| Động từ | Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---------|----------|-------|
| `say` | `say (that) + clause` | He **said that** he was busy. |
| `tell` | `tell + object + (that) + clause` | He **told me that** he was busy. |

> Không dùng `tell` nếu thiếu người nghe: ❌ He told that he was busy. → ✅ He said that he was busy.

---

## Quy Tắc Lùi Thì

Khi động từ tường thuật ở quá khứ (`said`, `told`), thì trong câu thường lùi một bước.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Present Simple | Past Simple |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect |
| Past Continuous | Past Perfect Continuous |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| may | might |
| must | had to |

### Ví dụ

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "I **work** here." | He said he **worked** there. |
| "I **am working**." | He said he **was working**. |
| "I **have worked** here." | He said he **had worked** there. |
| "I **went** home." | He said he **had gone** home. |
| "I **will call** you." | He said he **would call** me. |

---

## Đổi Đại Từ Và Trạng Từ

| Direct | Reported |
|--------|----------|
| I / me / my | đổi theo người nói |
| you / your | đổi theo người nghe |
| here | there |
| now | then / at that time |
| today | that day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week | the week before / the previous week |
| next month | the following month |

---

## Khi Không Cần Lùi Thì

| Trường hợp | Ví dụ |
|------------|-------|
| Sự thật vẫn đúng | She said water **boils** at 100°C. |
| Lời nói vừa mới xảy ra hoặc còn đúng | He said he **is** outside. |
| Động từ tường thuật ở hiện tại | She says she **likes** the plan. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| He told that he was tired. | He said that he was tired. / He told me that he was tired. | `tell` cần tân ngữ chỉ người nghe. |
| She said me she was busy. | She told me she was busy. | `say` không đi trực tiếp với `me`. |
| He said he is tired. | He said he was tired. | Với lời nói trong quá khứ, thường lùi thì. |
| She said she had seen me tomorrow. | She said she would see me the next day. | `tomorrow` đổi thành `the next day`; `will` đổi thành `would`. |
