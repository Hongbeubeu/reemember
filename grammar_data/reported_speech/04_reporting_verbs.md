# Reporting Verbs — Động Từ Tường Thuật

> **Cấp độ:** B2 · **Tần suất sử dụng:** Cao — giúp diễn đạt chính xác thái độ của người nói: khuyên, cảnh báo, hứa, đề nghị, phủ nhận, thừa nhận...

## Vì Sao Cần Reporting Verbs?

Thay vì luôn dùng `said` hoặc `told`, reporting verbs cho biết **mục đích** của lời nói.

| Direct Speech | Reported Speech |
|---------------|-----------------|
| "You should see a doctor." | She **advised me to see** a doctor. |
| "I didn't break it." | He **denied breaking** it. |
| "I'll help you." | She **promised to help** me. |

---

## Nhóm 1: Verb + Object + To + V

| Verb | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|------|------|-------|
| advise | khuyên | The doctor advised me to rest. |
| remind | nhắc | She reminded him to call his mother. |
| warn | cảnh báo | They warned us not to go near the river. |
| invite | mời | He invited me to join the meeting. |
| persuade | thuyết phục | I persuaded her to apply for the job. |
| encourage | khuyến khích | The teacher encouraged us to try again. |

---

## Nhóm 2: Verb + To + V

| Verb | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|------|------|-------|
| agree | đồng ý | He agreed to help us. |
| offer | đề nghị giúp | She offered to carry my bag. |
| promise | hứa | They promised to arrive on time. |
| refuse | từ chối | He refused to answer the question. |
| threaten | đe dọa | She threatened to call the police. |

---

## Nhóm 3: Verb + V-ing

| Verb | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|------|------|-------|
| admit | thừa nhận | He admitted making a mistake. |
| deny | phủ nhận | She denied taking the money. |
| recommend | khuyến nghị | They recommended booking early. |
| suggest | gợi ý | He suggested going by train. |

---

## Nhóm 4: Verb + That + Clause

| Verb | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|------|------|-------|
| explain | giải thích | She explained that the train was late. |
| insist | khăng khăng | He insisted that he was right. |
| complain | phàn nàn | They complained that the room was noisy. |
| claim | tuyên bố | The company claimed that the product was safe. |
| announce | thông báo | They announced that the flight was cancelled. |

---

## Một Số Cặp Dễ Nhầm

| Ý muốn nói | Dùng đúng | Ví dụ |
|------------|----------|-------|
| Khuyên ai làm gì | `advise + object + to V` | She advised me to study more. |
| Gợi ý cùng làm gì | `suggest + V-ing` | She suggested studying together. |
| Hứa làm gì | `promise + to V` | He promised to call. |
| Phủ nhận đã làm gì | `deny + V-ing` | He denied stealing the wallet. |
| Xin lỗi vì việc gì | `apologise for + V-ing` | She apologised for being late. |
| Buộc tội ai làm gì | `accuse + object + of + V-ing` | They accused him of lying. |

---

## Lỗi Thường Gặp

| Sai | Đúng | Giải thích |
|-----|------|------------|
| She suggested me to go. | She suggested going. / She suggested that I go. | `suggest` không dùng `object + to V`. |
| He denied to take the money. | He denied taking the money. | `deny + V-ing`. |
| They advised that I should to wait. | They advised me to wait. | `advise + object + to V`. |
| She promised helping me. | She promised to help me. | `promise + to V`. |
